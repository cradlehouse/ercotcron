## The question

Can the method land about $150k of cleared CRRs in a single monthly auction,
and does the extra capacity make money?

A ten-month blind walk-forward (Dec 2025–Sep 2026) says only about 19–23% of
authorised dollars get spent when limits sit at full value. It also says the
screen with a record lands only about $50k a month. This book tests the
gross-up and a widened screen out of sample.

## The rules

These are the same blind rules as the walk-forward:

- **Clearing basis:** the AUG, SEP and OCT 2026 monthly auctions.
- **Value:** the median of the 11 monthly mean peak spreads from Nov 2025 to
  Sep 2026, built from `node_day_spread`.
- **Fade gate:** Sep 2026 must be at least 25% of the median, and at least 7
  of the 11 months must be positive.
- **Excluded:** counterflow (the basis must be at least 2¢).
- **Tier A (core):** 54 bids, $464k authorised. Hub or load-zone leg; the
  median is at least 2× the basis; limit = median.
- **Tier B (extension):** 183 bids, $228k authorised. Any other peak OBL path
  whose median is at least 1.5× the basis; limit = median ÷ 1.25. Trimmed to
  the widest value-to-basis margins until the expected landed amount covered
  the $150k target.
- **Size:** half the market's average bought MW across the basis auctions,
  capped at 100 MW.
- **Total authorised:** $692k. Expected landed: about $150k (A about $107k, B
  about $43k). The plausible range is $105k–$215k.

## Grading

**At NOV results (by Oct 22): how much lands?** A bid fills when its limit is
at least the path's clearing price. For obligations that price is source minus
sink nodal shadow price. That rule matched every one of 160k+ bids across
three auctions.

Report, per tier: fills, dollars spent, and spent ÷ authorised.
**Pass: $100k–$200k landed in total.**

**At NOV settlement (early December): does it pay?** Use day-ahead payout on
the filled bids at their sealed MW.

**Pass: Tier B's payout exceeds its cost.** Tier A is graded too, but Tier B
is the question: if B loses, the real-money books stay Tier-A-only, which is
about $50k of capacity.
