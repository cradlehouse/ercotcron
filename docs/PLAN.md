# Shadowprice — The Path to Revenue, From Scratch

**One page of orientation, then the plan. Assumes: no network, no warm intros,
no capital beyond small operating costs. One founder + the machine + Steve.**

---

## What we are selling (only two things at first)

1. **Auction workflow tools** — the bid sheet + constraint alerts. For small
   ERCOT CRR trading shops. $250–750/mo. This is the product a real trader
   already priced at "$1k/mo without blinking."
2. **Episodic valuation work** — wind-downs, exits, diligence. $15–75k per
   event. Steve's own wind-down is prototype deal #1.

Everything else we've discussed (credit reports for banks, audit tools,
attestation, other markets) is **deferred** until the two above produce cash
and credibility. The free monthly Scorecard is marketing for both.

## The one number that matters

**First paying customer by October 31.** (Kill test: zero by December 15 →
this becomes a maintained side product, not a business.)

## Honest revenue expectation, from scratch

- **2026 (Sep–Dec):** $1–8k total. The goal is proof, not money.
- **2027:** $60–150k (subscriptions + 1–2 episodic events). Panel's higher
  numbers assumed warm networks; this assumes none.

  desk-scale otherwise.

## Standing rules (already decided, encoded here so nothing re-litigates them)

Neutral path — no buy-side exit-intel, ever. Per-holder data only to that
holder. No attested forward-value letters until multi-holder validation +
counsel + E&O. No "IOSCO-aligned" claims. Two-month holdout on all research.
Marks always shown beside the auction clear. Append-only everything.
